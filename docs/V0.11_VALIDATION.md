## Submission boundary

All v0.11 build/validation scripts preserve the operator boundary:

- no GOAI portal upload or final submit;
- no Gmail/email search/read/send/modify;
- no external account write;
- OpenAI live validation only after explicit `--include-openai` opt-in and user-controlled configuration.

Actual competition submission and confirmation remain user-manual only.
